Luna XP skin for Y2Reborn.

Images (title bar, buttons, taskbar, start button, controls, wallpaper) were drawn
from scratch with code for this skin and are released under CC0 1.0 (public domain).
The look is inspired by early-2000s desktop styling; it contains no Microsoft images,
icons, logos or fonts.

Fonts: DejaVu Sans Condensed (Regular, Bold, Bold Oblique) are included in fonts/ under
the DejaVu / Bitstream Vera free licence. See fonts/DejaVu-LICENSE.txt.

In the copy shipped with Reborn the three fonts are subsets of the originals (Latin, Greek and Cyrillic;
Bold Oblique ASCII only for the start button), made with pyftsubset to keep the update small.
